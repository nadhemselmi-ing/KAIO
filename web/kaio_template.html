How KAIO decides

Runs onboard every 10 seconds

Four steps, a few hundred lines of code, no training. Every input is something a real satellite already has onboard.

Step 1

Estimate what each image is worth

Quick-look entropy (texture) × land fraction from an onboard land/water mask, × 4 for fire alerts. w = p · (0.1 + 0.9·land) · (H/8)³

Step 2

Score every action

Process, transmit, offload or keep. Score = growth of three debts (storage backlog, battery below its eclipse floor, heat above 45 °C) minus V × value delivered, per second of resource used.

Step 3

Safety guard can veto

An independent check, like flight-software FDIR: no job starts if the battery would fall below the energy needed to survive the next eclipse, or the computer would end above 45 °C. Only fire alerts may use the margin up to the 60 °C hardware limit.

Step 4

Log a one-line reason

Every started job carries a reason built only from onboard data, so operators on the ground can audit each decision.
