## Production notes
- **Screen captures.** Run `streamlit run app.py` and pick a real day in the sidebar. Record the *Orbit* tab (the 2D map shows real scenes coloured by entropy, with VIIRS fire alerts), the *Subsystem telemetry* tab, the *Pareto frontier* tab and the *Live decision feed* tab. Record at 1920×1080. The 2D map needs internet for its coastlines.
- **Static figures.** `paper/figures/fig3_realdata.png`, `fig4_telemetry.png` and `fig7_cumulative.png` are rendered at 300 dpi.
- **Pacing.** If a read-through runs over 120 s, trim the roadmap line first. Keep the real-data segment; it is the strongest part of the story.
- **Wording check.** Say "real orbit, real NASA data, simulated spacecraft". The power, thermal and compute models are simulated, and the payload sizes and costs are assumptions.
